Cementerio de Marcas

Spanish-language documentaries about companies and brands that had it all and disappeared: what happened, which decisions sank them, and what we can learn.

Watch on YouTube

The channel

Each video tells the story of a fallen brand —Blockbuster, Kodak, Nokia and many more— in an 8 to 12 minute documentary. The same story is also published in short parts on YouTube Shorts and TikTok.

Images are real, freely licensed photographs (Wikimedia Commons, Pexels, Pixabay), credited in each video's description. Narration uses a synthetic voice and scripts are prepared with the help of AI, so videos are labeled as altered or synthetic content where applicable, and every video is reviewed by hand before it is published.

The Cementerio de Marcas application

"Cementerio de Marcas" is also the name of the internal tool the channel owner uses to prepare and publish their own videos. It is not a public service: only the channel owner uses it, with their own account.

  • YouTube: it uses the YouTube Data API (youtube.upload scope) solely to upload the channel's videos and thumbnails to the owner's own account and schedule their publication. Videos are always uploaded as private, with a publish time at least 24 hours later, so the owner can review them in YouTube Studio before they go live.
  • TikTok: it will later use the TikTok Content Posting API to send the short videos to the channel's own TikTok account.
  • It does not access other people's data, does not read comments, subscribers or analytics, and does not sell or share any data.
